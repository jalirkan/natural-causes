# School — the cast, toy direction

`python tools/art/cast.py --cast tools/art/boards/SCHOOL-CAST.md` then `pnpm art:intake -- --act=school` then `pnpm art:pack`.
One model, one style line, one seed: that is what keeps them looking like one set.

style: soft global illumination, clean matte plastic materials, cute, Pixar-like, plain neutral grey background, centered, full body
negative: text, watermark, blurry, deformed, extra limbs, logo, crowd, photo, realistic

- player-school: small kid figure, round head, one big tuft of hair sticking straight up, t-shirt and shorts
- dodgeball: red rubber dodgeball with a mischievous cartoon face, a ball, an object, no people
- homework: stack of paper homework sheets with a grumpy cartoon face, an object, no people
- hall-monitor: tall skinny kid figure wearing a diagonal orange fabric sash across the chest like a school hall monitor, stern, arms folded
- clique: exactly four kid figures standing glued together shoulder to shoulder in a row, whispering
- substitute-teacher: tired adult teacher figure in a cardigan holding a clipboard, slouching
- boss-gym-teacher: big burly gym teacher figure with a whistle on a cord, short shorts, tube socks, hands on hips
